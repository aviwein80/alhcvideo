# Autumn Lake Healthcare brand ad — plan and storyboard

Source IDs (C1…C12, T0…T4, S1…S9) refer to `docs/research.md`. This is the
second cut: faster, scored, voiced, with an illustrated tour of a center.

## Concept: "Good care. Good times."

The company's own copy pairs two ideas in almost every sentence: professional
standards and personal warmth ("clinical excellence and compassionate care",
"havens of hospitality", "here you'll find family"). The film states that
belief, then **opens the doors and shows it**: a single camera move through an
illustrated center where staff are doing the work, and it pays off with the
tagline that says the same thing in four words.

The logo's tree is the visual thread. It grows leaf by leaf in the opening,
sits as a watermark behind type, hangs as the sign over the front desk, lends
its leaf to bullets and bursts, and reassembles for the sign-off.

## Audience

- **Primary:** adult children and spouses choosing short-term rehabilitation
  or long-term care for a family member in Connecticut, New Jersey, Maryland,
  West Virginia or Wisconsin.
- **Secondary:** hospital discharge planners and referral partners;
  prospective team members.
- **What they should leave with:** this looks like a place where people are
  cared for and enjoy themselves, there is one near me, and here is where to look.

## Format

- 1920×1080, 30 fps, 56.0 s (1,680 frames), H.264 MP4 with stereo audio.
- **Beat grid:** 120 BPM, so 1 beat = 15 frames and 1 bar = 2 seconds. The
  film is 28 bars. Every cut, camera move and accent sits on the grid, and the
  score, effects and voice are generated from the same cue sheet
  (`src/edit.json`), so picture and sound cannot drift apart.
- **Sound:** an original score in D major, a sound-effects stem and a
  voiceover, all generated by the scripts in `scripts/audio`. The score dips
  under each spoken line.

## Guardrails applied

- People appear only as illustration. No photographs of people, no stock
  footage, nothing photoreal, no real residents or staff.
- The rooms show the services the company names (rehabilitation, long-term
  care, hospitality). Nothing depicts a medical result.
- No statistics, outcomes or testimonials (none are published by the company).
- Care is attributed to "each center"; the management-company disclaimer is on
  the end card.
- The logo is shown only in its original colors on a light background. The
  tree over the front desk is decoration, not the logo lockup.
